# Residential Lease Agreement (sample)

This is a made-up lease so you can try the app. Delete it from the sidebar whenever you like.

**Landlord:** Harbor Lane Properties Ltd.
**Tenant:** Sam Rivera
**Property:** Flat 4B, 27 Harbor Lane, Riverside

## 1. Term

The lease starts on 1 April 2026 and ends on 31 March 2027. Either party may end the lease early with 60 days' written notice, but the tenant pays a break fee of one month's rent if they leave before 1 October 2026.

## 2. Rent

Rent is 1,250 euros per month, due on the 1st of each month by bank transfer. Payments more than 5 days late incur a late fee of 40 euros. Rent may increase by at most 3% when the lease is renewed.

## 3. Deposit

The tenant pays a security deposit of 2,500 euros (two months' rent) before moving in. The landlord returns it within 30 days of the end of the lease, minus the cost of any damage beyond normal wear and tear.

## 4. Utilities

Water and building heating are included in the rent. The tenant pays electricity, internet and the TV licence directly.

## 5. Pets

One cat or small dog is allowed with written permission from the landlord. An extra pet deposit of 300 euros applies. No other animals are allowed.

## 6. Repairs and maintenance

The landlord handles structural repairs, plumbing, heating and appliances that came with the flat. Urgent repairs (no heat, no water, leaks) must be started within 48 hours of being reported. The tenant replaces light bulbs and smoke alarm batteries and keeps drains clear.

## 7. Subletting

The tenant may not sublet the flat or list it on short-term rental sites. Guests may stay up to 14 nights in a row.

## 8. Moving out

The tenant must return all 3 keys and leave the flat clean. A final inspection takes place on the last day of the lease. The tenant should give a forwarding address for the deposit refund.
